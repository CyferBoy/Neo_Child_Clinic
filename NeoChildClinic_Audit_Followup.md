## Updated Conclusion

This is a substantial, well-executed remediation pass — 11 of the original 18 findings are fully resolved, 2 are effectively resolved with a narrow and clearly reasoned exception, 1 has its concrete harm mitigated without the underlying duplication being removed, and only 3 (the Composable→Room-entity coupling, the `BiometricLockManager` global-state access, and the `SyncUploader` manual-construction item) remain untouched. Two things stand out about *how* it was done, beyond just the count:

1. **The fixes follow the original recommendations almost exactly** — method names (`resolveSessionOrDefer`, `processGroup`), the `Result<Unit>`-returning `reauthenticateWithPassword` signature, and the `PatientDocument` mapping approach all match what Section N proposed, rather than being independently-arrived-at alternate fixes. The `SyncEntityDescriptor` registry went further than requested (consolidating 4 switches, not 2).
2. **Nothing was fixed superficially.** In each case checked, the interface change is backed by a real implementation change underneath (e.g., `InventoryRepository` didn't just gain method signatures — the domain services were actually rewired to call them), and the original code's hard-won incident documentation (RLS timing, FK ordering) was preserved through the refactor rather than lost.

The remaining open items are consistent with a team that prioritized the architectural/testability fixes (DI boundaries, DAO bypasses, SDK leakage) over the cosmetic-but-wide one (16 files' worth of Room-entity-typed Composable parameters) and the lowest-severity one (`BiometricLockManager`) — a reasonable sequencing, not a sign of an incomplete effort. The newly-flagged `BackupRepositoryImpl` gap (Finding 19) is the one remaining instance of the "repository with no interface" pattern the rest of the codebase has now eliminated, and is the natural next item to close for full consistency.
